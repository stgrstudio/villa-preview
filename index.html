How much is the deposit?

30% holds your dates, by transfer or card link; the rest is paid on arrival.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancelling is free up to 14 days before arrival; after that the deposit is kept.

When can I check in and out?

From 14:00, out by 11:00; direct guests may stay to 14:00 when the villa is free.

Do you arrange airport transfers?

Pick-up from Denpasar airport is free on direct stays of 3 nights or more.

What is included in the price?

Breakfast, housekeeping, pool care, Wi-Fi, drinking water, tax and service.
